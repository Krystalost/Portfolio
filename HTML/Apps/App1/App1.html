Blinding Lights

The Weeknd

Released as the second single from his critically acclaimed fourth studio album After Hours, "Blinding Lights" is an electrifying 1980s-inspired synth-pop and synthwave anthem. Driven by pulsating drum machines, euphoric synthesizers, and a relentless tempo, the track explores themes of loneliness, intoxication, and the desperate yearning for a lover through the neon-drenched streets of a night-time city. Combining retro nostalgia with modern pop production, it became a record-breaking global phenomenon and one of the defining tracks of modern music history.
