Interstellar

Christopher Nolan

Set in a near future where Earth is slowly dying from catastrophic dust storms and widespread crop failure, the story follows Cooper, a former NASA test pilot turned farmer who is recruited for a vital interstellar mission to save humanity. Leaving his family behind, he joins a team of astronauts to journey through a newly discovered wormhole near Saturn in search of habitable new worlds across the cosmos. Exploring themes of sacrifice, the relativity of time, and the profound reach of human connection, the film is an awe-inspiring space epic about the relentless drive for survival against all odds.
